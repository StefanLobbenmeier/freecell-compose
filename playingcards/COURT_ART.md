Court illustrations are cropped and recolored to a muted red, blue, and gold palette from the public-domain Vector Playing Cards deck:
https://code.google.com/archive/p/vector-playing-cards/

PNG source: https://github.com/hayeah/playing-cards-assets (commit below).
That repository's MIT license is preserved in COURT_ART_LICENSE.txt.
Only the central illustration is used; indices, paper, and number cards are drawn by the app.
